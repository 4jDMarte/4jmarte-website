Company Overview

About 4jMarte Industrial Automation Ltd

4jMarte Industrial Automation Ltd is an Irish automation and digital manufacturing consultancy delivering specialist engineering and technology company specializing in Industrial Automation, Cybersecurity, Digital Transformation, Operational Technology (OT), and Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC) Support. Founded in Philippines 2003 and re-established and 2020, the company delivers engineering, validation, maintenance, compliance, and technology services to the pharmaceutical, biotechnology, petrochemical, utilities, and manufacturing sectors across Ireland, Europe, Singapore, and the Philippines.

With more than 20 years of international industry experience, 4jMarte provides expertise in ABB 800xA, Emerson DeltaV, AVEVA PI System, Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES), Power Management Systems (PMS), industrial networks, data integration, and smart manufacturing solutions. Our services support the complete project lifecycle, from concept development and system architecture through implementation, commissioning, qualification, maintenance, optimization, and continuous improvement. Our portfolio includes support for JG Summit Petrochemical Corporation's ABB 800xA Power Management System, electrical substation automation projects, and ABB France Cellier process package systems, including Drum Decanting Systems, Lube Oil & Additives Blending Systems, Pigging Systems, Filling Lines, Packaging Systems, and associated utility infrastructure. Delivered services have included design reviews, installation, configuration, commissioning, Site Acceptance Testing (SAT), Installation Qualification (IQ), Operational Qualification (OQ), and Performance Qualification (PQ) support for Pfizer, Guerbet, and MSD facilities in Ireland, as well as calibration, preventive maintenance, disaster recovery planning, troubleshooting, and emergency breakdown response. We remain committed to delivering secure, reliable, and innovative engineering solutions that enhance operational performance, regulatory compliance, business continuity, and Long Term Support (LTS).

Implementing Purdue Model Segmentation & Zero Trust for AVEVA PI & DeltaV Environments

Bridging Operational Technology (OT) and Enterprise IT requires strict adherence to IEC 62443 standards without compromising real-time control loop determinism. By isolating Level 1–3 control networks behind DMZ data diodes and hardened historians, life sciences and petrochemical facilities achieve full GAMP 5 compliance alongside modern analytics visibility.
