Company Overview

About 4jMarte Industrial Automation Ltd

4jMarte Industrial Automation Ltd is an Irish automation and digital manufacturing consultancy delivering specialist engineering services tailored to high-compliance, mission-critical production environments.

We provide comprehensive engineering solutions spanning Industrial Automation, Distributed Control Systems (DCS), Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES), Industrial Networking, Cybersecurity, Process Information Systems, GMP Validation, and Digital Transformation programs.

Primary Service Domains

  • Industrial Automation
  • Distributed Control Systems (DCS)
  • Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES)

  • Digital Transformation
Specialist Services Engagement

Engineering Partnership Proposal: Generic Industry 4.0

4jMarte Industrial Automation Ltd proposes to partner with industrial manufacturing partners to provide specialist Systems Automation Engineering services supporting site Emerson DeltaV Distributed Control Systems (DCS), Fisher-Rosemount PROVOX/RS3 systems, Process Information Systems, Manufacturing Information Systems, OT infrastructure, and GMP-regulated manufacturing operations.

Proposed Engagement Key Objectives

Operational Reliability

Improve overall site operational stability and system reliability.

Validated GMP Status

Maintain strict compliance and validated GMP regulatory status.

Production Performance

Support and optimise site production throughput & performance.

Minimise Downtime

Reduce automation-related downtime through proactive engineering.

Cybersecurity Posture

Improve industrial OT security posture and threat defense.

Extend System Lifecycle

Maximise long-term value and extend legacy system lifecycle.

Future Modernization

Support future automation modernization and system upgrade initiatives.
